Questions

Before you get access.

A few practical answers about the digital product and its future delivery flow.

Is this a physical product?

No. Product 01 is a digital habit-tracking product.

What devices will it support?

The tracker is designed as a responsive web experience for desktop, tablet and phone screens.

Will I receive the tracker immediately after payment?

The final delivery timing and access flow will be configured with the payment and entitlement system before launch.

Will the public website contain the tracker file?

No. The launch architecture keeps the actual product outside the public marketing site and uses a protected paid-access flow.

Will there be more CLADHABITS products?

Yes. Product 01 is the first product in the planned CLADHABITS digital toolkit.

Protected access

Purchase first. Access second.

The public CLADHABITS website will not expose the tracker file. Paid access will be handled through the payment platform’s purchase flow.

1

Purchase through the official CLADHABITS checkout.
Your payment is processed by the configured payment provider.

2

Sign in using the purchase details.
Your account is checked against the CLADHABITS purchase record before the private product is delivered.

3

Open the purchased product.
The tracker is delivered/accessed only through the configured paid-product delivery flow.

Security note: a browser app cannot be made secretly encrypted while still being runnable by the browser. We therefore will not rely on hidden URLs, front-end passwords, or fake JavaScript “encryption” as the access control.
